# Image Credits

The launch version of melwongdance.org uses **no photographic imagery of Mel Wong**. We made this choice deliberately:

1. The known public photographs of Mel Wong (UCSC archive, Westbeth, press obituaries) are © UC Regents, © Westbeth Artists Housing, or © press wire — none are clearly licensed for reuse on a third-party memorial site.
2. The risk of copyright violation outweighs the visual benefit on a small foundation site.
3. Editorial typography + restrained ink-stroke decoration carry the page without infringing.

If the Foundation or family contributes original or licensed photographs, they will be added here with full attribution.

## Future contributions

To submit a photograph for inclusion, the contributor must provide:

- **Source / Origin** of the image (who took it, where, when)
- **Licensing** (Creative Commons license, public domain status, or written permission)
- **Caption text** including required attribution

Send to the Foundation address listed in the site footer.
